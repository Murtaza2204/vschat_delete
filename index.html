Delete Your VSChat Account

If you would like to delete your VSChat account and associated data, you can submit an account deletion request to the VSChat support team using the steps below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to support@vschat.net.
  2. Use the subject line VSChat Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the mobile phone number associated with your VSChat account so we can identify the account and verify the request.

After receiving your request, the VSChat team will verify it and manually process the account deletion.

Request Account Deletion

This opens Gmail with the address and subject filled in. If Gmail doesn't open automatically, email support@vschat.net directly with the subject VSChat Account Deletion Request.

What data is deleted

When an account deletion request is verified and processed, VSChat will delete the user's account and associated data that is maintained in our systems and is eligible for deletion. This may include:

  • VSChat account and profile information
  • Phone number associated with the account
  • User-associated database records
  • User-uploaded images and other media, where applicable
  • Other account-associated information that is eligible for deletion

Data that may be retained

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required for:

  • Legal or regulatory requirements
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Technical purposes such as backups

Any retained information will be handled according to applicable privacy requirements and deleted when it is no longer required.

Processing time

Account deletion requests are manually reviewed and processed after verification. You may be contacted if additional information is required to verify your request.

Note: Deleting a VSChat account is different from deleting a single message or image from a chat.

Removing a message from the app does not automatically mean that all copies of that data are removed from every backend system. Account deletion requests are handled separately, through the process described on this page.

Contact

For questions about account deletion or privacy, contact support@vschat.net.
